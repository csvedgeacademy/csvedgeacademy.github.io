📘 What is this Master Class about?

This free master class is designed to provide clear and practical understanding of Computerized System Assurance (CSA), as introduced by the FDA. You will learn how CSA optimizes validation activities using a risk-based approach, without eliminating Computerized System Validation (CSV).

👥 Who should attend?

  • CSV & GxP professionals
  • Quality Assurance & Compliance professionals
  • IT professionals working on GxP systems
  • Freshers looking to enter CSV / CSA roles
  • Anyone confused about CSV vs CSA

🧠 What you will learn

  • What is CSA and why FDA introduced it
  • Difference between CSV and CSA
  • Risk-based testing and assurance concepts
  • How CSA fits into existing CSV lifecycle
  • Real examples from Pharma & GxP systems
